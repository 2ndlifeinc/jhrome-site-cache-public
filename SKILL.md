# jhrome site cache

jhrome reads `data/bindings.json` and `data/notes.json` from this installed skill on every lookup, under the bindings and notes it learned on this machine. What it learns is saved on this machine only. Reinstalling brings the newest data; jhrome needs no restart.

The data is generated by `site-element-registry` (`scripts/action-cache/ship.mjs`) from replay-confirmed bindings only, and holds no screen that shows who is signed in.
